# Expense Policy

Harbor Lane Logistics is a made-up company. This document is sample data.

## What can be claimed

Employees can claim reasonable costs that are needed for work. This includes
travel, accommodation, meals while travelling, and client meetings.

Personal items, fines, and alcohol without a client present cannot be claimed.

## Limits

- Meals while travelling: up to 60 USD per day.
- Hotel: up to 180 USD per night. Higher rates need approval before booking.
- Flights: economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is
  allowed for longer flights.
- Taxi or ride share: allowed when public transport is not practical.

## Approval

Any single expense above 500 USD needs written approval from your manager
before the money is spent.

## How to claim

Submit claims in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense. Attach a
receipt for every item above 25 USD. Claims without receipts are rejected.

Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary run.
