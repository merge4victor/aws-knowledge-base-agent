# Leave Policy

Harbor Lane Logistics is a made-up company. This document is sample data.

## Annual leave

Full time employees get 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Part time employees get the same amount in proportion to their hours.

Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Carried over days
must be used by 31 March or they are lost.

Leave requests must be sent to your manager at least 10 working days before
the first day of leave. Requests for more than 10 days in a row need approval
from the department head.

## Sick leave

Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required for any absence longer than 2 days in a row.

Tell your manager before 9:00 on the first day you are sick.

## Parental leave

Employees with at least 12 months of service get 16 weeks of paid parental
leave. Leave can start up to 4 weeks before the expected due date.

## Public holidays

Public holidays are paid days off and do not count as annual leave.
