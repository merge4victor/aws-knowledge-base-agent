# Remote Work Policy

Harbor Lane Logistics is a made-up company. This document is sample data.

## Who can work remotely

Office based employees can work from home up to 3 days per week after they
finish their 3 month probation period. Warehouse and driver roles are not
eligible because the work must be done on site.

## Core hours

Remote employees must be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 local time on
working days. Outside core hours the schedule can be agreed with your
manager.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, a monitor, and a headset. Employees get a one
time allowance of 300 USD for home office furniture. Keep the receipts and
claim it through the finance portal.

## Working from another country

Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per year.
It needs approval from HR at least 4 weeks in advance because of tax and
insurance rules.

## Security

Always use the company VPN when you work outside the office. Do not use
public wifi without the VPN.
